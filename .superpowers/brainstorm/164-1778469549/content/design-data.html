数据层新增

需要新增的 DAO 查询

1. 训练类型分布 — 按类型统计训练次数

SELECT activityType, COUNT(*) as count FROM cardio_entries WHERE dateEpochDay BETWEEN ? AND ? GROUP BY activityType

＋ strength 类型从 workout_logs 关联 strength_exercises 统计

2. 有氧时长趋势 — 按日/周聚合

SELECT w.dateEpochDay, SUM(c.durationMinutes) as totalMinutes FROM cardio_entries c JOIN workout_logs w ON c.workoutLogId = w.id GROUP BY w.dateEpochDay ORDER BY w.dateEpochDay

3. 力量动作历史趋势 — 按动作名查每次训练重量

SELECT w.dateEpochDay, e.name, MAX(s.weightKg) as maxWeight, SUM(s.weightKg * s.reps) as volume FROM strength_sets s JOIN strength_exercises e ON s.exerciseId = e.id JOIN workout_logs w ON e.workoutLogId = w.id GROUP BY w.dateEpochDay, e.name ORDER BY w.dateEpochDay
